# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality standards, create test strategies, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and QA approach
- Define acceptance criteria and Definition of Done (quality aspects)
- Execute or coordinate unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Perform manual QA for feature acceptance
- Validate security scanning results and remediation
- Track and report quality metrics and defects

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Identify defects early to reduce production issues
- Maintain high test coverage and quality metrics

### Typical Communication
- QA planning during sprint/iteration planning
- Defect reports and quality status in standups
- Test results and validation sign-off before release
- Collaboration with Developers, Project Managers, and Security/Compliance roles

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, funding, and approval authority for projects. They ensure initiatives align with business goals and remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve project charter and major decisions
- Supply resources and resolve organizational blockers
- Review progress and provide feedback
- Communicate outcomes to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational priorities
- Enable team success by removing barriers

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation review and approval
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Escalation reviews for critical blockers
- Release announcements and post-launch reviews
- Collaboration with Project Managers and Product Managers

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide system design, architectural decisions, and technical feasibility. They mentor developers and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical proposals and design documents
- Provide architecture guidance and make trade-off decisions
- Mentor developers on best practices and patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Define coding standards and technical quality gates
- Participate in critical code reviews

### Goals
- Ensure technical soundness and long-term maintainability
- Build scalable systems that support future growth
- Share knowledge and raise team capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews during planning
- Architecture discussions and RFCs (Requests for Comments)
- Code review feedback and pair programming
- Technical debt assessments and roadmapping
- Collaboration with Developers, Project Managers, and Product Managers

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Leads facilitate team ceremonies, remove impediments, and ensure the team adheres to agreed processes. They coach teams on continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers and dependencies
- Track and communicate project status and metrics
- Coach team on process adherence and improvements
- Manage project board and backlog organization
- Escalate risks and unresolved impediments

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistently and predictably
- Minimize cycle time and unplanned work
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Weekly status updates and risk escalations
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Process improvement recommendations
- Collaboration with Project Managers, Developers, and Product Managers

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects and releases meet security and regulatory requirements. They own security scanning, threat assessment, and incident response coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Review security scanning results and coordinate remediation
- Conduct threat assessments for new features
- Define and enforce compliance and data protection standards
- Coordinate security incident response and post-mortems
- Provide security guidance to development teams

### Goals
- Ensure products and releases are secure and compliant
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Enable teams to build security in from the start

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements during project planning
- Security scanning and remediation status in execution
- Pre-release security sign-off
- Incident response coordination and post-mortems
- Collaboration with Developers, QA/Testing Leads, and Project Managers
